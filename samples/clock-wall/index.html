Sample 47 · Clocks as pixels

Clock Wall

How it's made: about a hundred glass clock faces, each with two hands turned by transform through the Web Animations API, aimed from a pose table drawn for each digit. A diagonal wave redraws the time every minute and a ripple plays every 15 seconds; it pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab.
