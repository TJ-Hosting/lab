Sample 11 · Flow ribbons

Streamline ink poster

How it's made: a Canvas 2D tracer follows a simplex-noise flow field, keeps each line clear of the rest and draws it as a tapered ribbon. A seed picks the poster and a new one draws in after a rest; it stops off-screen or in a hidden tab.
