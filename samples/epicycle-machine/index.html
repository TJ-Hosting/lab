Sample 49 · Circles that draw

Epicycle Drawing Machine

How it's made: A Fourier transform turns the outline's 512 points into up to 300 circles laid end to end, and the tip of the last one redraws the shape on Canvas 2D. Time turns the circles, and it pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab.
