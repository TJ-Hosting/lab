The mark, assembling

How it's made: the mark is one inline SVG; the globe's rings scale and turn into place and the TJ arrives as seven scanlines, each clipped to the letterform. Everything moves with transform and opacity through the Web Animations API, so Replay simply rebuilds the timeline.
