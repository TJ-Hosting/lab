Tear the Glass Curtain

How it's made: a grid of points joined by threads, moved by Verlet physics and drawn on Canvas 2D; a thread stretched too far snaps, and a slice cuts the threads it crosses. A breeze moves it when idle; the loop pauses off-screen and in a hidden tab, and with reduced motion the curtain is already parted.
