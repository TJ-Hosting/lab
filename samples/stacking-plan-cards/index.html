Sample 22 · Sticky stack

Sticky stacking plan cards

How it's made: every card is position: sticky, and one CSS scroll timeline shrinks a card as the next one slides over it, with only transform and opacity. Only scrolling moves it; with reduced motion, or in a browser without scroll-driven animations, the cards just stack.
