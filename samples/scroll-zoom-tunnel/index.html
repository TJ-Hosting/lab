The scroll tunnel

How it's made: about forty glass tiles sit in one CSS 3D scene, and each has its own animation tied to the scroll bar, flying from far away to past the camera with only transform and opacity. Nothing moves unless you scroll; with reduced motion, or a browser without scroll-driven animations, you get a still grid instead.
