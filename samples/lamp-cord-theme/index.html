Pull-Cord Theme Lamp

How it's made: a Verlet rope on Canvas 2D holds the lamp, and pulling its cord spreads the new theme as a circle (a View Transition, or a plain switch where it is missing). A breeze sways it; it pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab, and hangs still with reduced motion.
