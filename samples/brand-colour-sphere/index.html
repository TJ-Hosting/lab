Sample 32 · Colour maths

The brand colour sphere

How it's made: an inline SVG of colour patches on a sphere, with hue running around it and lightness from white to black, worked out in OKLCH; each frame a quaternion turns every patch and its outline is redrawn. Drag to spin it with some inertia, or leave it to drift; it pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab.
