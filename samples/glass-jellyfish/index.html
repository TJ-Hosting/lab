Glass jellyfish

How it's made: a Canvas 2D bell pulses as it swims, and each glowing tentacle is a chain of points that trails behind it on simple rope physics. It follows your pointer with a lag, wanders alone when you stop, and pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab.
